<sha> chore: seed calculator   ← baseline

`aic` split one file's three unrelated concerns — a `fix`, a `feat`, and a
`style` change, each in its own hunk — into three atomic Conventional-Commits,
staging each hunk separately (`git add -p` style). SHAs vary per run; the
subjects are deterministic in offline fixture mode.
